Setup guide

  1. Check your device

    Android has a full app. On iPhone, use the web version at snakzy.app and add it to your Home Screen.

  2. Tap “Open Snakzy”

    Use the button on this page so your sign-up is linked correctly.

  3. Create your account

    Sign up with your email. If you already have an Eneba account, you can log in with the same email.

  4. Pick a game inside Snakzy

    Always start the install from inside Snakzy, not directly from the Play Store, or your progress may not count.

  5. Play with tracking on

    Open the game through Snakzy, keep tracking turned on, and press stop in Snakzy when you finish.

  6. Reach the milestones

    Coins come from playtime and in-game goals. Offers can take 24 to 48 hours to show as pending before they’re confirmed.

  7. Redeem your coins

    Once you reach the minimum, choose a gift card, or PayPal where it’s available in your region.

Common questions

Is it free?

Yes. Snakzy is free to download and play.

Do I get cash?

Rewards are mainly gift cards, with PayPal available to some users. Amounts vary and nothing is guaranteed.

Why didn’t my progress count?

The most common cause is installing a game outside Snakzy or playing with tracking off. Follow steps 4 and 5 above.

Who runs Snakzy?

Snakzy is developed by Eneba (Helis Play UAB), based in Vilnius, Lithuania. Support is available inside the app.
